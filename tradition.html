Alaska & Tradition

Fortitude carried across generations.

Sled dogs have long connected people, places, and possibilities across the North.

More than a race

Dog teams helped make travel, trade, communication, exploration, and emergency response possible across vast winter landscapes.

Modern endurance racing carries echoes of that history. Our role is to tell these stories with context, respect, and an understanding that northern traditions are varied, living, and deeply connected to place.

Community

Dog teams helped connect remote settlements and move essential supplies.

Adaptation

Knowledge of weather, snow, terrain, and animal behavior shaped survival.

Resilience

Travel demanded preparation, judgment, cooperation, and perseverance.

Living heritage

Tradition continues through stories, mentorship, racing, recreation, and daily care.
