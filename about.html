About Us

Storytelling rooted in responsibility.

We exist to help audiences understand the knowledge, care, and partnership behind sled dog life.

Our mission

To elevate thoughtful, truthful stories about sled dog care, training, Arctic endurance, and Alaskan tradition.

We believe the strongest public awareness comes from showing the whole picture: preparation, judgment, veterinary collaboration, rest, nutrition, relationship, heritage, and respect for each dog as an individual.

Welfare first

Health and well-being come before performance.

Truthful storytelling

We show the work behind the race, not only the spectacle.

Respect for tradition

We honor the knowledge and resilience rooted in the North.

Education with empathy

We encourage informed discussion that strengthens care and understanding.
