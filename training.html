Training & Preparation

Built through patience, not pressure.

Strong teams grow through gradual conditioning, clear communication, and trust.

Training with purpose

Preparation should strengthen the dog physically and emotionally.

Responsible programs increase distance and difficulty gradually, watch recovery closely, adapt to weather and trail conditions, and respect each dog’s readiness.

01

Foundation

Socialization, equipment familiarity, handling, recall, and calm routines.

02

Conditioning

Short, manageable sessions that build fitness and confidence.

03

Teamwork

Learning positions, pacing, communication, passing, and group rhythm.

04

Readiness

Longer efforts paired with recovery monitoring and informed decision-making.
