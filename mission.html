Our Mission

Honoring sled dogs through care, connection, education, and tradition.

Arctic Paws Collective brings audiences closer to the dogs, mushers, veterinarians, handlers, and communities behind responsible mushing.

Why we exist

We believe sled dogs deserve to be understood not only as endurance athletes, but as individuals whose health, confidence, relationships, and lifelong well-being matter.

Through thoughtful storytelling and education, we highlight responsible canine care, purposeful training, the bond between mushers and their teams, and the Alaskan traditions that continue to shape the trail.

Sled Dog Care

Promoting excellent health, nutrition, veterinary support, recovery, emotional well-being, and lifelong care.

Training & Preparation

Sharing responsible approaches to conditioning, safety, confidence, communication, and readiness.

Musher Connection

Celebrating trust, observation, consistency, and partnership between mushers and their dogs.

Alaska & Tradition

Preserving stories of resilience, transportation, community, history, and northern heritage.
