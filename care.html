Canine Care

Endurance begins with wellness.

Responsible care is daily, individualized, preventive, and attentive.

What quality care looks like

Every dog brings a different age, metabolism, temperament, history, and role to the team.

Nutrition & hydration

Fueling strategies should reflect workload, climate, body condition, recovery needs, and veterinary guidance.

Preventive medicine

Routine exams, vaccinations, parasite prevention, dental care, screening, and early intervention form the backbone of health.

Recovery & rest

Rest days, sleep, warm shelter, paw care, and post-exercise monitoring allow the body to adapt safely.

Emotional well-being

Healthy working relationships depend on social connection, predictability, confidence, and appropriate enrichment.

Trail monitoring

Behavior, appetite, hydration, gait, attitude, and willingness to work help guide real-time decisions.

Retirement & lifelong care

A dog’s value does not end when racing does. Responsible programs plan for every stage of life.
